# Little Secret Overview

## About this project

My family likes to play one night werewolf and because we're all locate in different locations, we play it online using this site called [Netgames](https://netgames.io/games/onu-werewolf/) to help facilitate the game. It's very streamlined and fits in seamlessly with our experience on FaceTime. Recently, I went to Japan with a few Wharton classmates and had a ton of fun playing a game called [Little Secret](https://little-secret.com/), which is a similar game to one night werewolf, but with a different theme and some different mechanics. I want to emphasize that my house had a ton of fun playing this game. It was a core bonding component of my house. After searching for a while, I couldn't find any online implementations of the game, so I decided to make my own!


You can play the game here:
 - [Little Secret Online](https://little-secret.scafejr.me)

Check out the rules here:
 - [Little Secret Rules](https://little-secret.scafejr.me/Little_Secret_US_FLYER.pdf)

It's multiplayer, so you may need a few devices to actually play this correctly.

I decided not to go with anything business related, because I have already built quite a bit that I use already. I figured that this would be a nice change of pace.
