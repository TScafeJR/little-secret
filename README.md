This game is an online version of
[Exploding Kitten's "Little Secret"](https://www.explodingkittens.com/products/little-secret)
card game.

Looking for a thrilling game to liven up your next family gathering?

Dive into the world of _Little Secret_, the social deduction card game that’s
all about bluffing and deception! Unmask the imposters among you or protect your
secret identity as the suspense builds with every turn. Perfect for game nights,
holidays, or any time you want to bring friends and family together for a dose
of friendly competition and laughter.

Easy to learn, quick to play, and endlessly entertaining, _Little Secret_ will
have everyone on the edge of their seats.
